abstract: |
  <p>Thiers lists the Church’s condemnations of sorcery. At pp. 139–140 of the 1697 printing, he lists papal bulls against sorcerers, including those of Leo X and Adrian VI for northern Italy. Lecky cites him in note 5, together with Spina and Madden, for the persecution in Italy.</p><p>The passage describes papal commissions, not any resistance: it contains no account of a rebellion.</p>

<h2>Pp. 139–140: Papal Bulls Against Sorcerers</h2>

<p class="passage-source">French: Paris, 1697 printing, pp. 139–140, summarised. The OCR dates are garbled in the scan’s text; the dates given below are the standard ones for these bulls and are not read from the page. <span class="cited-passage">Highlighted</span>: the content Lecky could be using.</p>

<div class="parallel">
<div class="original" lang="fr">
<p>En 1484, le Pape Innocent VIII, par sa Bulle <em>Summis desiderantes affectibus</em>, donna un ample pouvoir au Père Henry Institoris et au Père Jacques Sprenger […] d’informer contre les Magiciens de la haute Allemagne. […] <span class="cited-passage">Le Pape Léon X en usa de même à l’égard d’autres Inquisiteurs, contre les Sorciers du diocèse de Bresce et de celui de Bergame</span> […]. <span class="cited-passage">C’est ce que fit aussi Adrien VI par sa Bulle <em>Dudum</em> […] à l’égard de l’Inquisiteur de Crémone, contre les Sorciers qui se trouvoient en certains cantons de la Lombardie.</span></p>
</div>
<div class="rendering" lang="en">
<p>In 1484 Pope Innocent VIII, by his Bull <em>Summis desiderantes affectibus</em>, gave ample power to Father Henry Institoris and Father Jacob Sprenger […] to inquire against the Magicians of upper Germany. […] <span class="cited-passage">Pope Leo X did the same for other Inquisitors, against the Sorcerers of the diocese of Brescia and that of Bergamo</span> […]. <span class="cited-passage">Adrian VI did likewise by his Bull <em>Dudum</em> […] for the Inquisitor of Cremona, against the Sorcerers found in certain cantons of Lombardy.</span></p>
</div>
