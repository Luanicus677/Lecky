abstract: |
  <p>Thiers wanted to prove that the Church had always condemned magic, so he lined up every pope and council he could find, from a Paris council of 826 (“magic and sorcery are certainly relics of paganism”) to the councils of Milan, Bordeaux, Mexico and Narbonne. In the middle of the list are the three papal bulls that launched the great witch-hunts of Germany and northern Italy. Lecky cites the page for the Italian persecutions; what it does not contain is the “absolute rebellion” against the inquisitors that Lecky mentions in the same note.</p>

<h2>Three popes against the sorcerers (p. 139)</h2>
<p class="passage-source">French: Paris, 1697 printing, p. 139, <a href="https://archive.org/details/bub_gb_sn7cWyefS5AC/page/n173/mode/2up">scan</a>. <span class="cited-passage">Highlighted</span>: what bears on Lecky’s note.</p>
<figure class="facsimile"><a href="../assets/img/ref/thiers-1697-p139.jpg"><img src="../assets/img/ref/thiers-1697-p139.jpg" alt="A printed French page listing the bulls of Innocent VIII (1484), Leo X (1521) and Adrian VI (1522) against magicians and sorcerers" loading="lazy"></a><figcaption>Thiers, <i>Traité des superstitions</i> (Paris, 1697), p. 139. <span class="credit">Scan: Internet Archive (Google Books) · public domain</span></figcaption></figure>
<div class="parallel">
<div class="original" lang="fr">
<p>En 1484. le Pape Innocent VIII. par sa Bulle <em>Summis desiderantes affectibus</em>, donna un ample pouvoir au P. Henry Institor &amp; au Pere Jacques Sprenger, Religieux de l’Ordre de saint Dominique, &amp; Inquisiteurs de la Foi Catholique, d’informer contre les Magiciens de la haute Allemagne, &amp; de les punir selon la grandeur de leurs crimes.</p>
<p>En 1521. <span class="cited-passage">le Pape Leon X. en usa de même à l’égard d’autres Inquisiteurs, contre les Sorciers du diocese de Bresse, &amp; de celui de Bergame</span>, comme il se voit par sa Bulle <em>Honestis petentium votis</em>.</p>
<p><span class="cited-passage">C’est ce que fit aussi Adrien VI. par sa Bulle <em>Dudum</em>, qui est du 20. jour de Juillet 1522. à l’égard de l’Inquisiteur de Crémone, contre les Sorciers qui se trouvoient en certains cantons de la Lombardie.</span></p>
</div>
<div class="rendering" lang="en">
<p>In 1484 Pope Innocent VIII, by his bull <em>Summis desiderantes affectibus</em>, gave ample power to Father Henry Institor and Father Jacob Sprenger, of the Order of St. Dominic and Inquisitors of the Catholic Faith, to proceed against the magicians of Upper Germany and to punish them according to the greatness of their crimes.</p>
<p>In 1521 <span class="cited-passage">Pope Leo X did the same for other inquisitors, against the sorcerers of the diocese of Brescia and that of Bergamo</span>, as is seen by his bull <em>Honestis petentium votis</em>.</p>
<p><span class="cited-passage">Adrian VI did likewise by his bull <em>Dudum</em>, dated 20 July 1522, for the Inquisitor of Cremona, against the sorcerers who were found in certain districts of Lombardy.</span></p>
</div>
</div>
<p>Henry Institor and Jacob Sprenger are the authors of the <a class="popup-ref" href="sprenger-malleus-maleficarum.html"><em>Malleus maleficarum</em></a>, which was printed with Innocent VIII’s bull as its preface. On the facing page Thiers carries the list on: the Council of Milan (1565), the French clergy at Melun (1579), and councils at Bordeaux, Mexico, Narbonne and Ferrara, all ordering magicians to be punished or excommunicated.</p>
