## What this is

A reading edition of William Edward Hartpole Lecky's major works, built page by page rather than published all at once. The design borrows its reading philosophy — <a href="https://gwern.net/design" class="popup-ref" target="_blank" rel="noopener">semantic zoom</a>, footnotes you can hover to preview, and links that pop up what they point to — from Gwern Branwen's gwern.net. The site's code is our own; the footnote and popup styling is adapted from gwern.net's stylesheet, which Gwern publishes under CC0/MIT licenses. The Wikipedia link icon is from [Font Awesome Free](https://fontawesome.com/license/free) (CC BY 4.0).

## Current status

The typography, layout, footnote system, and hover-popup mechanism are built and working. Real text has started landing: the Introduction to *Rise and Influence of Rationalism in Europe* is sourced, OCR-cleaned, and proofread against the scan. Volume II is complete as far as Lecky’s text goes (Chapter IV Part II, Chapter V and Chapter VI, pages 1–409), so the whole of the *Rationalism* text is up; the index is not transcribed. Volume I (pages 1–447: Chapters I–III and Part I of Chapter IV) is complete. Seventy-two [source pages](ref/index.html) are up: [Grillandus](ref/grillandi-de-sortilegiis.html), [Garinet](ref/garinet-histoire-de-la-magie.html), [Bodin](ref/bodin-demonomanie-des-sorciers.html), the [Codex Theodosianus](ref/codex-theodosianus-ix-16.html), [Sprenger’s Malleus](ref/sprenger-malleus-maleficarum.html), [Gerson](ref/gerson-de-probatione-spirituum.html), [Spina](ref/spina-quaestio-de-strigibus.html), [Hecker](ref/hecker-epidemics.html), [Dalyell](ref/dalyell-darker-superstitions.html), [Scot](ref/scot-discovery-of-witchcraft.html), [Burt](ref/burt-letters-north-of-scotland.html), [Hutchinson](ref/hutchinson-historical-essay.html), [Madden](ref/madden-phantasmata.html) and [Thiers](ref/thiers-traite-des-superstitions.html), all for works cited in Chapter I; and [Middleton](ref/middleton-free-inquiry.html), [Simancas](ref/simancas-de-catholicis-institutionibus.html) [Chrysostom](ref/chrysostom-de-sacerdotio.html), [Augustine on lying](ref/augustine-contra-mendacium.html), [Tertullian](ref/tertullian-de-spectaculis.html) (also [on baptism](ref/tertullian-de-baptismo.html) and the [*Apology*](ref/tertullian-apologeticum.html) and [*De anima*](ref/tertullian-de-anima.html)), [Minucius Felix](ref/minucius-felix-octavius.html) and [Luther](ref/luther-de-servo-arbitrio.html), [Aquinas](ref/aquinas-summa-supplement.html), [Agobard](ref/agobard-de-imaginibus.html) and [Augustine’s *City of God*](ref/augustine-de-civitate-dei.html), for a few notes in Chapters II–IV. Most notes of Chapters II–IV are not yet linked to source pages. Hover a citation in the notes to preview its source, or a foreign quotation's "English" button for a translation.

There is no clean transcription of this title anywhere (it isn't on Project Gutenberg or Wikisource), so each chapter comes from a 160-year-old library scan's raw OCR, run through an automated cleanup pass and then read through by hand before publishing. That means it goes up one chapter at a time rather than all at once.

Next steps, in order:

1. Continue with Volume II (Volume I, printed pages 1–447, is done).
2. Identify every work, document, and figure Lecky cites, and build reference pages for them — starting with the source texts (the witchcraft manuals, pamphlets, and letters), each transcribed in full where we can find them.
3. Wire citations in the main text to hover-previews and click-throughs into those reference pages.
4. A cross-reference map of how it all connects.

## On the text

Lecky's writing and the primary sources he cites are in the public domain. Where we rely on a particular library scan or edition, we credit it on the relevant page.
