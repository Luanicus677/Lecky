Swinden, An Enquiry into the Nature and Place of Hell

An Anglican clergyman proves that hell is in the sun

Author
Tobias Swinden, M.A., “late Rector of Cuxton in Kent” (title page). The Enquiry was first published in 1714; a French translation, Recherches sur la nature du feu de l’enfer, et du lieu où il est situé, appeared in 1757. The second edition adds a supplement on the eternity of hell and “the Rev. Mr. Wall’s Sentiments of this learned Work”, presumably William Wall, author of the History of Infant Baptism.
Edition used
Second edition, London 1727 (Internet Archive natureofhell00swin): title page = n4; p. 129 = n156, matching Lecky’s “p. 129”; chapter VII from the OCR (about p. 132 = n159).
Cited in
Rationalism, ch. III, note 151 (“Swinden, p. 129”).
Status
P. 129 and the title page read on the images; the chapter VII extract from the OCR only.

Lecky quotes “an Anglican clergyman” for the boast that no other religion had a hell described with such particularity as Christianity’s. He does not say what made the clergyman notable. Swinden’s book argues, at length and with astronomical calculations, that the sun is the material hell: its chapter VII opens, “Having conjectured that the Body of the Sun is the Tartarus or Local Hell, it will be expected that some Reasons be laid down to justify” it. The sun, he reasons, is large enough and hot enough to hold the damned of all ages. Lecky’s quotation is accurate apart from one word (“particularity” for “Particularities”).

“Not with the same Particularities … in any other Religion” (p. 129)

English: London 1727, p. 129, scan. Highlighted: Lecky’s quotation.

The Christians Heaven is clearly another thing, and of a quite different Nature from the Elyzian Fields of the ancient Heathen, and from the sensual Paradise of the present Mahumetan; and the Hell described in the Gospel is not with the same Particularities to be met with in any other Religion, that is or hath been in the whole World. Knowing therefore, on the one hand, these Terrors of the Lord, we persuade Men; and having, on the other, these Promises, we exhort them to cleanse themselves from all Filthiness both of Flesh and Spirit, perfecting Holiness in the Fear of the Lord.

“The Body of the Sun is the Tartarus or Local Hell” (chapter VII)

English: chapter VII, “Reasons for the aforesaid Conjecture”, from the OCR (image n159; page not read).

Having conjectured that the Body of the Sun is the Tartarus or Local Hell, it will be expected that some Reasons be laid down to justify, and shew the Probability, at least, of the Assertion. Now the first that I shall bring for this Purpose, shall be drawn from the Nature of the Body of the Sun …
