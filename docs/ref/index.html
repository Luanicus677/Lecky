Sources

The books, pamphlets, and documents Lecky cites, each on its own page with the passage he used, a translation where it isn’t in English, and a link to a scan of the edition the text comes from. Every source page lists the places in Lecky that cite it, so you can move in either direction.

  • Grillandus, De Sortilegiis1 passage
    Paolo Grillandi’s handbook on sorcery, 1530s. Quaestio III, §12, in Latin and English, with Lecky’s quotation compared against the 1545 and 1592 editions. Cited in Rationalism, ch. I, note 1.
  • Garinet, Histoire de la Magie en France1 passage
    Jules Garinet’s history of sorcery in France, 1818. The preface’s survey of ancient laws against sorcerers (pp. xiii–xiv), in French and English, compared with Lecky’s summary, including the sorceress “Lemia”. Cited in Rationalism, ch. I, notes 3, 4, 9, and 14.
